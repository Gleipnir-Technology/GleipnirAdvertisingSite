The Problem

Mosquito Control Is Hard

Not because the work is complicated. Because the knowledge is scattered. Observations live in notebooks. Experience lives in people's heads. When staff leave, knowledge walks out the door.

Scattered observations

The Solution

What Nidus Is

Capture System

A system for capturing and retaining operational observations. Every observation recorded and preserved. Nothing lost to notebooks or memory.

Shared Record

A shared record that supports coordination across people and time. Everyone sees the same operational picture. New staff inherit existing knowledge.

Learning Foundation

A foundation for learning what actions reduce mosquito production. Patterns emerge over time. Knowledge compounds with each season.
